The Platform

Built to read the game.

Four modules, one suite. Everything streams into a single sideline view, so the data is already an answer by the time you look down.

Module 01

Athlete Monitoring

Every athlete on your roster streams eight physiological signals from their wearable, synced to the game clock. Baselines are set per athlete and per sport — a linebacker and a point guard are never judged on the same curve.

Heart RateHRVSpO₂RespirationCore TempPerspirationDistanceRecovery Rate
Module 02

Camera Auto Sync

Your sideline camera becomes a sensor. Positional tracking is aligned to each athlete's vitals second by second, so the engine sees the sprints not finished and the ground not covered — and feeds all of it into the fatigue score.

Module 03

In-Game Decisions

This is more than a fatigue meter. Our AI learns the correlation between each athlete's fatigue — from the vest and the camera — and their actual performance on the floor: execution, decision-making, the quality of their reps. It times every substitution to the exact moment that maximizes the performance you get for the energy spent, while weighing how costly that athlete is to take off. Two players at the same fatigue are not the same sub.

HOLDING UPPerformance steady — stay in.
SLIPPINGOutput dropping — line up a sub.
COSTING YOUFresh legs win the trade — go.
Module 04

Analytics & Review

Two things, after the final whistle. Each athlete gets a report of how their biometrics moved across the game — turned into the specific drills that will push the vitals holding them back. And the whole game gets reviewed: were the AI's substitution calls right, what did they cost or save, and what should change next time. Every game makes the next decision smarter.

How It Works

From roster to sideline in one afternoon.

No installation project, no analyst on payroll. Here's the full path from signing up to making your first data-backed substitution.

01

Build Your Roster

Pick your sport, name your squad, and add starters and bench. Athletrix sets per-athlete baselines so fatigue is measured against each athlete's own normal — not a league average.

02

Go Live On Game Day

Wearables stream vitals the moment the clock starts. Camera auto sync aligns position data to the same timeline, and the live dashboard updates with every possession.

03

Coach With Answers

When fatigue crosses a threshold you get the alert, the reason, and the recommended swap. Take it or leave it — the call is always yours, now with data behind it.

Common questions

Do we need special hardware?

Athletrix reads from standard consumer and team wearables — heart-rate straps and sport watches your athletes may already own. Camera auto sync works with an ordinary sideline camera setup.

Can we try it before buying?

Yes — the live demo is open to everyone, no account needed. It runs a complete game with two example teams so you can see the fatigue engine and substitution calls exactly as a coach would.

What counts as a season?

One competitive season for your sport, including pre-season training. Off-season conditioning for the same squad is included at no extra cost.

We run a district or multi-club program. What then?

Create an account and our team will tailor a rollout — multi-squad licensing, shared analytics across clubs, and onboarding for your whole coaching staff.

The Problem

Fatigue is invisible from the bench

Coaches read body language and box scores. Neither shows a heart that isn't recovering between possessions. By the time fatigue is visible, it's already cost you.

The Build

An AI engine tuned by athletes

We built the fatigue model around eight live vitals and calibrated it sport by sport — then wrapped it in a dashboard designed to be read in a thirty-second timeout.
